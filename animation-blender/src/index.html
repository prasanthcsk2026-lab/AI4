How the timeline works

Groups (Group…): one weight for a whole arm, leg, spine…; groups and bones inside them multiply (arm 60 % × hand 50 % = hand at 30 %).

FK (Bone…, or click a joint): weight 0–200 % of the clip, adjust in degrees about each axis (the axis names say what each one does), timing offset.

IK (IK…, or click a square / ring handle): move / rotate hips, spine, chest, neck, head, shoulders, hands, feet; swivel elbows and knees; curl fingers; pin, hold and pull; group IK and IK controllers.

+: optional master tracks: playback speed, moving speed, cycle speed and bar reach.

In a lane: click to add a point (the magnet snaps it to the grid), drag it, drag the small ring between two points to bend the curve, double-click a point or click a value to type it, right-click a point to delete it. Shift-drag box-selects; arrows nudge; Ctrl+C / V copy and paste; Ctrl+Z / Y undo / redo.

Tracks: drag a track's bottom edge to make it taller; Alt + wheel zooms its values. Time: Ctrl + wheel zooms, Shift + wheel scrolls, the bar at the bottom scrolls and zooms, Fit shows everything.

Viewport: Q select · W move · E rotate · K key · F frame · Space play.

Bake / Project

Bake & replace turns everything you did (automation, groups, symmetrize, IK) into the clip's own frames. The clip keeps playing the baked motion and the timeline starts clean. It is kept in this browser; Revert brings the original back.

Save to project stores the baked clip in this artifact's database. Then ask Claude to apply it, and it is written into the project files (it replaces the clip there).
